# sessions: the brain's short-term memory

Each session that touches the brain appends a short digest to `sessions/YYYY-MM-DD.md`
(format in `procedures/capture.md`). The weekly dream reads every digest here, folds what matters
into notes, people, projects, decisions and learnings, then moves the digest to `sessions/dreamed/`.

- Dated filenames are allowed here, as in `output/`: these are episodes, not topics.
- Nothing here is a source of truth until the dream has compiled it.
- Never delete; `dreamed/` is the record of what has been consolidated.
