# Contributing

Thank you for helping. This kit stays small on purpose.

## Good contributions
- Fixes to the scripts (standard library only, Python 3.9 or later).
- Clearer wording in the procedures or the map.
- Adapters for other tools (notes apps, mail clients), kept in their own folder with a short README.
- Lessons from running your own brain: open an issue with what happened and what you changed.

## Rules
- Never include personal data, even your own. Use the fictional example (Alex Carter) or invent new
  fictional data.
- Keep `brain/` and `example/` in step: procedures, scripts, templates and the writing standard are
  identical in both.
- Follow `brain/notes/writing-standard.md` for prose: short sentences, British spelling, no en or
  em dashes.
- Scripts must be read-only by default, or refuse unknown options and support `--dry-run`.
